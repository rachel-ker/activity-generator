Quick tips for grown-ups & printing
  • Try 5–10 playful minutes. Say unfamiliar words first; practise one new spelling at a time.
  • Some common words sound out as expected, like can. Others have a tricky part, like said. Help your child sound out the regular parts and explain the tricky part.
  • Print on A4, portrait, actual size (100%), with browser headers/footers off. Bingo needs a board per player plus calling cards.
  • Windows/Chromebook: Ctrl+P. Mac: Cmd+P. Choose your printer or Save as PDF.
  • Android/iPhone/iPad: try the browser menu or Share → Print when available. If the file opens as a preview and buttons do not work, use a browser that supports local HTML and JavaScript.
  • Interactive games need no printing. Tap cards, drag or tap pictures, or select both ends of a word. Session scores clear on reset or reload.
